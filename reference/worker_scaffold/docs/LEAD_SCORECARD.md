# Lead scorecard

Do not use follower count as a client-count estimator. A low-follower business can have strong offline traffic, referrals or repeat customers.

Score prospects 0–2 on each item:

1. Appointment-based service
2. Uses Instagram/WhatsApp for bookings
3. No obvious booking system
4. Repeated posting / business is active
5. 40+ likely appointments per month
6. Average ticket likely >= 100 MAD
7. No-show/coordination pain likely
8. One owner can say yes quickly
9. Services/prices are standardizable
10. Capacity exists for more or better-kept appointments

Priority:
- 16–20: contact immediately
- 12–15: good prospect
- 8–11: only if convenient
- <8: skip

Good initial categories:
- piercings
- lashes / nails
- hair treatments
- barbers
- beauty salons
- tattoo studios where booking is lawful and appropriate
- coaches / studios
- massage/spa businesses

Avoid first:
- businesses with complicated medical data
- heavily regulated services
- multi-location chains
- businesses requiring deposits/payment integrations on day one
